My approach

Spiritual curiosity, grounded in your own agency.

This is a space to explore what matters to you with openness and care. Readings are offered for reflection and personal insight—not as guarantees about the future or another person's private thoughts.

You are always free to ask questions, keep what feels useful, and make your own decisions.

“Let a reading be a mirror for reflection—not a substitute for your own voice.”

How booking works

No automatic payment or calendar is required to get started. Your appointment is only confirmed after you and the guide agree on the details.

STEP 01

Choose a reading

Select the service that best matches the question or theme you want to explore.

STEP 02

Send an enquiry

WhatsApp opens with your selected reading and preferred contact details, ready for you to review and send.

STEP 03

Agree on the details

Discuss availability, session format, duration, and payment privately before confirming.

Begin a conversation

What would you like to explore?

Share a little about the reading you are interested in. Your message will be prepared for WhatsApp; you can edit it before sending.

Please avoid sending highly sensitive personal information in your first enquiry. Session availability and payment arrangements are discussed directly before a booking is confirmed.
